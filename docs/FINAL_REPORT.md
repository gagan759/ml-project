# Final report — Tomato Care: two models for five conditions

Tomato Care diagnoses five tomato-leaf conditions (Healthy, Leaf Miner, Magnesium Deficiency, Nitrogen Deficiency, Spotted Wilt Virus) with two independently validated models: a **deep-learning** model (5 ConvNeXt-Tiny networks) and a **classical machine-learning** model (handcrafted colour and texture features, no neural network). Both were chosen on out-of-fold cross-validation data with the same rules and scored once on the same held-out test photos.

Generated by `scripts/39_final_report.py` from the result files; every number below is copied from `experiments/results/`. Method: `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` §6c–6e. Audit history: `docs/AUDIT.md`.

> Final pipelines fixed on out-of-fold data (scripts/36, rules in docs/AUDIT.md §6) and scored here once on the test photos. The test photos had been used for reporting in earlier rounds (v1, v2, v2.1), never for a choice. Classical model (scripts/43) scored once on the test photos by scripts/44, 2026-11-19 23:55.

## 11. Limitations

- **Five conditions only.** The dataset's Early Blight (62 photographs), Late Blight and Potassium Deficiency (9) were too hard to recognise reliably in the earlier eight-condition study, so the app does not diagnose them; their test photos are used only to check that they are refused (sections 1 and 7).
- **Outlier exposure covers known unknowns.** The deep model learned to refuse the dataset's other three conditions; a tomato disease it has never seen may still receive a diagnosis. The classical model had no such training and relies on its threshold and its feature-distance gate.
- **Domain shift.** All training photos come from one phone, two districts and leaves on paper. PlantVillage photos (another lab) and stress tests probe this, but field photos with complex backgrounds were not available.
- **Test-set reuse.** The test photos were reported in v1, v2 and v2.1 (never used for a choice). Every choice here was fixed on out-of-fold data with rules written before training; the test score is a repeated look.
- **Small test set.** Differences of a few points are within the bootstrap intervals; read the paired comparisons, not single numbers.
